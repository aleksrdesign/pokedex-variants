Charmender

Fire

Charmander is a Fire-type Pokémon native to the Kanto region, measuring about 60 centimeters and weighing an average of 8.5 kilograms. This small, bipedal reptile with bright orange scales is instantly recognizable by the glowing flame that burns constantly at the tip of its tail.

Far from being a mere ornament, this biological spark serves as a direct indicator of the Pokémon's vital signs: its intensity and color fluctuate depending on the Pokémon's health, energy level, and emotions.
