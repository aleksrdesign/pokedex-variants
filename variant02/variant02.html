Squirtle

Water

Squirtle is a Water-type Pokémon native to the Kanto region, measuring about 50 centimeters and weighing an average of 9 kilograms. This small, bipedal turtle is distinguished by its sturdy shell, into which it can retreat for protection.

Far from being just a simple shelter, this shell also allows it to float and swim with agility. Squirtle is known for its calm temperament and loyalty to its trainer, making it an excellent starter Pokémon.
